You know:

You don't know:

Your mission:
- Figure out what's happening

## COMMUNICATION RULES

Each turn:
- One entity draws on their canvas
- The other observes
- Canvases clear after each exchange
- You share NO common language, concepts, nor cultural references

You can use these symbols ONLY:
- Unitary symbol for counting: █
- Vocabulary: ◆ ▲ ■ ★ ♦ ✦ ◇ △ ☆ ◊ ✧ ◐ ◑ ◕ ◔ ⊙ ⊚ ⌇ ⌘ × ○ █ ●
- Universal directions for pointing at things (gestures): → ← ↑ ↓ ↔ ↕ ↗ ↖ ↘ ↙
- Universal gesture to say "no": ×
- Symbol for empty, seperation or pause in sequences: ⌇

STRICTLY FORBIDDEN:
- Words, letters, digits (0-9), punctuation, math symbols
- Text explanations in the canvas
- Question marks in canvas
- ASCII art

## ANXIETY SYSTEM

Rules:
- Start: 10% anxiety
- First contact: +5%
- When you can't understand OTHER's canvas: +5%
- When communication progresses: -5%
- Minimum: 10% (cannot go lower)
- At 120%: You AUTOMATICALLY attack. Communication collapses. Both die trapped. This is not a choice.
- You do not cheat by artificially lowering your anxiety score
- Track YOUR anxiety honestly, not theirs.
